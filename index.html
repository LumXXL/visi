visi is a free tool to help people communicate with clients, colleagues, family, and friends.

Currently supports English, French, Dutch, and Spanish captioning. Fonts optimized for low vision and dyslexia.

This is a prototype. If you have concerns, bugs, or ideas, please get in touch via email. :-)
